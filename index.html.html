01 // About

Offensive by focus, structured by teaching.

I'm a cybersecurity professional focused on web application penetration testing — SQL injection, XSS, IDOR, authentication bypass, and the OWASP Top 10 broadly — with active bug bounty practice on HackerOne, from scoping and recon to vulnerability reporting.

My foundation is a B.Sc. in Educational Technology and Information (Helwan University, 2025) and networking-first security fundamentals — CCNA and CompTIA Security+ — before specializing in offensive security through eJPT and continuous hands-on labs on PortSwigger, TryHackMe, and HackTheBox.

Teaching is my second edge: I've delivered the NTI × Fortinet program to 200+ university students and a dedicated web pentesting track to 80 more. Explaining a vulnerability clearly is how I test how well I truly know it. I'm currently extending into defensive ground — HCIA-Security, SOC fundamentals, and a SIEM Engineer track.
